# WK — AI-Native Community & Professional Ecosystem

WK is an AI-native community super-platform connecting people, knowledge, opportunities, services, organizations, and trusted collaboration in one ecosystem.

## Strategic Position

WK combines community, professional networking, learning, experts, services, partnerships, opportunities, organizations, marketplace transactions, sponsorships, advertising, and AI intelligence.

The strategy is Saudi-first, then GCC, Arab, and global. Arabic and English are first-class languages with Arabic-first UX.

## Architecture

WK starts as a modular monolith with explicit domain boundaries and a path to service extraction when justified. Core engines include Identity, Community, Events, Learning, Marketplace, Opportunities, Organizations, Payments, Settlement, Messaging, Notifications, Search, Matching, Trust, Moderation, Advertising, Analytics, AI, Audit, and Compliance.

Google Cloud is the recommended primary cloud with Saudi-region deployment where appropriate. The platform remains cloud-agnostic and can support Saudi sovereign infrastructure for sensitive workloads or disaster recovery.

## AI

The repository is designed for human and AI development. Execution modes are Discuss, Plan, Build, and Audit. AI agents operate under project instructions, skills, guardrails, security rules, and risk-based approval.

## Security and Compliance

Security is designed in from the start. OWASP ASVS Level 2 is the baseline, with stronger controls for identity, payments, administration, AI actions, and sensitive data. Saudi laws and regulations are treated as applicability requirements.
