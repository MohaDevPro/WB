# MVP Scope

The MVP establishes the ecosystem foundation.

Core:
- Authentication and professional identity
- Community and membership
- Groups and discussions
- Events
- Expert/service discovery
- Basic opportunity and partner discovery
- Search
- Notifications
- Admin and moderation
- Trust/verification foundation
- AI assistant and recommendation foundation
- Arabic/English
- Security, audit, analytics, and compliance foundations

Full LMS, payments, escrow-like settlement, advanced matching, and autonomous agents follow through staged releases.
