# Identity and Professional Profiles

Each person has one platform identity that can participate in multiple roles.

A professional profile may include biography, skills, experience, education, certifications, portfolio, services, expertise, languages, availability, location, interests, organization memberships, verification, trust badges, and selected activity.

Visibility is user-controlled. Explicit privacy choices override defaults.

Roles are additive: a person may be a member, expert, provider, mentor, partner, and organization administrator simultaneously.
