# Community Engine

The Community Engine is generalized and independent of external messaging platforms.

It supports communities, groups, channels, discussions, posts, comments, reactions, polls, membership, roles, moderation, reports, events, and contribution signals.

WhatsApp, Telegram, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, and Instagram are initially external links only.

Future federated communities may have separate branding, membership, rules, content, and governance while sharing the WB ecosystem.
