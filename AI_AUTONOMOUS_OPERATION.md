# AI Autonomous Operation

WK is architected for Level 5 autonomy but launches around Level 2–3.

0 Human only
1 AI assists
2 AI recommends
3 AI executes approved workflows
4 AI executes bounded autonomous workflows
5 AI coordinates autonomous multi-agent workflows

Autonomy is permission-based. Each capability has a risk class, allowed tools, data scope, approval requirement, rate limit, audit requirement, and rollback or escalation path.
