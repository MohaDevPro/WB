# Platform Governance

Governance is separated from daily administration.

Recommended structure:
- Platform Governance
- Product & Ecosystem
- Trust & Safety
- Technology & Security
- Community Management
- Marketplace Operations
- Content & Learning
- Compliance & Privacy

Permissions are scoped. No one operational role should control identity, financial settlement, security administration, and audit evidence simultaneously.

The final legal entity and formal committee structure remain intentionally deferred.
