# Risk Register

| Risk | Impact | Initial response |
|---|---|---|
| Regulatory changes | High | Compliance monitoring and applicability matrix |
| Payment/escrow licensing | High | Regulated providers and legal review |
| Marketplace fraud | High | Verification, trust, moderation, fraud controls |
| AI unsafe actions | High | Capability policies, approval gates, audit |
| Data breach | Critical | Defense in depth, encryption, monitoring, least privilege |
| Premature microservices | Medium | Modular monolith first |
| Low marketplace liquidity | High | Stage supply and demand acquisition |
| Community abuse | High | Hybrid moderation and governance |
| Vendor lock-in | Medium | Provider abstraction |
| Poor Arabic UX | High | Arabic-first design and localization |
| Documentation drift | Medium | Documentation review in CI/change process |
