# Saudi Compliance Applicability Matrix

| Area | Relevance | Engineering response |
|---|---|---|
| PDPL | High | Privacy, consent, rights, minimization, retention, security |
| Cybersecurity | High | Risk-based security governance and controls |
| E-commerce | High | Consumer-facing transparency and controls |
| VAT | High where taxable | Tax-aware billing and reporting |
| E-invoicing | High where applicable | Billing supports applicable ZATCA requirements |
| Payments | High | Regulated provider architecture |
| Advertising | Medium/High | Clear commercial disclosure |
| Cloud/data residency | Risk dependent | Region-aware deployment and data classification |
| Consumer protection | High | Terms, refunds, complaints, disclosures |

This is an engineering planning artifact. Final legal applicability must be validated against the legal entity and actual business activities.
